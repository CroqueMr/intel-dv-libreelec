## Opt1 - Lower Dolby Vision GPU load

- **Black-bar optimization:** skips unnecessary processing outside the active picture. **Not limited to FEL**-applies to supported DV profiles with active-area metadata. Around **11% lower rendering load** in tested letterboxed sections.
- **libplacebo optimization:** removes redundant filtering work without changing image quality. **Not limited to FEL**, with around **12% lower rendering load** in tested full-frame sections.

**Combined: approximately 16% lower rendering load overall, up to 24% in letterboxed sections.**

Measured on **i7-11390H**.
